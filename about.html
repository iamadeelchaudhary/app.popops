Built for Android Developers.

PopOps was founded on the principle that developer-to-user communication shouldn't require a constant sprint cycle or app store review delays.

The Problem

Push notifications have 40% opt-out rates. App updates take 24-48 hours for review. Critical alerts can't wait for a user to check their notifications.

The PopOps Solution

Real-time, in-session messaging. When you publish a message in our dashboard, it appears in your app within milliseconds of the next sync.

Our Mission

We aim to be the standard communication layer for Android indie developers and startups. By providing a lightweight SDK and a powerful dashboard, we help teams maintain high engagement and ensure user safety through mandatory update prompts and maintenance warnings.
