Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

Welcome to our application. Your privacy is important to us, and this Privacy Policy explains how our app collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our services.

1. Introduction

This application is designed for Android developers to manage in-app messages, updates, and communication within their own applications. We are committed to protecting your personal information and ensuring transparency about how data is handled.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect limited information to provide and improve our services. This may include:
• Basic account information such as email address and username when you sign up.
• App-related information such as app name and package name when registering your app.
• Usage data including interactions with features to improve performance and user experience.

We do not collect sensitive personal information such as passwords of other services, personal files, contacts, or media from your device.

3. How We Use Information

The collected information is used only for the following purposes:
• To provide core app functionality and services.
• To allow developers to manage and configure their registered apps.
• To improve app performance, stability, and user experience.
• To communicate important service updates or support information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.

4. Data Storage & Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect your data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. While we strive to use commercially acceptable means to protect your data, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Our app may use trusted third-party services for analytics, authentication, or hosting to ensure reliable functionality. These services may collect limited technical information required for their operations and follow their own privacy policies.

6. Data Sharing

We do not share personal information with third parties except:
• When required by law or legal process.
• To protect rights, safety, and security of users or the service.
• With trusted service providers who assist in operating the app under strict confidentiality.

7. User Control

You may choose to stop using the app at any time. If you wish to delete your account or associated data, you may contact us using the details provided below.

8. Children’s Privacy

This app is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If such data is discovered, it will be removed promptly.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the app after updates indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, you can contact us at:
Website: https://cocodestudio.com
Email: support@cocodestudio.com

By using this application, you agree to the terms outlined in this Privacy Policy and consent to the collection and use of information as described above.
